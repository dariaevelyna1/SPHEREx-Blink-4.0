# Education and local impact

## What exists (and is tested)

- **Learn view** (`learn.html`) in English and Romanian: 8 steps, one idea and one question each, a "hunt the mover" game, an
  is-it-a-planet checklist, a glossary. Romanian is chosen with the EN/RO buttons, `?lang=ro`, or the browser language. Checked by
  `tests/test_learn.mjs` (same keys in both languages, placeholders, "[unverified]" kept on the history line).
- **Classroom worksheet** (`learn-worksheet.html`, `?lang=ro|en`): one printable page for a high-school physics or astronomy lesson
  (about 40 minutes, groups of 3), questions tied to Learn steps 3, 4, 6 and 7, a small calculation (pixels per year to arcseconds per year,
  with the numbers of synthetic field A), a team activity and a teacher's key. Linked from the last Learn step.
- **Team activity**: three roles (blinker, spotter, recorder), swapped each round, scores compared.

Every picture used is SYNTHETIC and says so; the worksheet says so in a box.
